01

What is a Stock & Share?

When a company needs money to expand (like building new factories or hiring engineers), it splits ownership into millions of tiny pieces called **Shares**.

lightbulb Key Concept: Market Cap

**Market Capitalization** = Total Shares × Current Share Price. It represents the total value of the company on the stock exchange.

02

Order Types: Market vs Limit

How do you actually buy or sell a share? You send an **Order** to the exchange through a broker or trading platform.

⚡ Market Order: Buys immediately at the best current available price. Best for beginners!
🎯 Limit Order: Sets an exact price limit. (e.g. "Only buy AAPL if price drops to $210").
03

Reading Stock Charts & Candlesticks

A candlestick chart shows price movements during a specific timeframe (e.g. 1 Day).

04

Golden Rules of Risk Management

Protecting your capital is more important than chasing get-rich-quick stocks!

  • check_circle **Diversify**: Never put all cash into 1 single stock.
  • check_circle **Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA)**: Invest a fixed amount monthly.
  • check_circle **Start with ETFs**: Index funds like SPY carry lowest risk.
